🧹 Job Data Cleanup

Review every job in your account and permanently delete any that don't belong — leftover test/import data, duplicates, anything that was never a real job created in JOBSMETRIX. This is a true delete, not an archive: it also removes the job's estimate, invoices, change orders, logs, and any SLA to-dos already generated for it.

🔗 PlannerXD Integration

Lets JOBSMETRIX push things (customer signed a proposal, SLA alerts, live dashboard numbers) into your PlannerXD Do First list and Weekly Pulse. Needs your PlannerXD account's own user ID — not your JOBSMETRIX login, a completely separate value from a separate account. To find it: open PlannerXD, look for your User ID (Settings/Account area), and paste it here.

📈 Supplier Cost Adjustment
